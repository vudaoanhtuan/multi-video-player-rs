Video Player user manual

Video Player opens a folder of camera recordings and plays all cameras at the same time of day.

Hotkeys

Shift + GJump to the time in the clipboard
TPause and copy the clock time

EscCancel or close a dialog, or stop the camera choice of the bbox capture
Cmd + OOpen a folder (Ctrl + O on Windows)

The hotkeys do not work while a dialog is open or while you type in a text box.

Open and close a folder

  • To open a folder, use "Open Folder…" in the menu button (three dots) or press Cmd + O.
  • The app reads the files directly in the folder. It does not read folders inside the folder.
  • A file name must have this form: camera-id_yyyyMMddTHHmmss.mkv. Example: floor-1-camera-2_20261019T213000.mkv. The time is the start time of the recording.
  • The app skips files with a different name and files that it cannot read. It shows the number of skipped files.
  • After a folder opens, all cameras are shown and playback is paused at the earliest time.
  • "Close Folder" in the menu button stops playback and closes all files.

The app only reads the video files. It does not change, move, or delete them. It does not play sound.

Camera grid

  • Each shown camera has one tile. The camera id is in the top left corner of the tile.
  • All tiles show the same time of day. This time is the clock time.
  • A tile is black when its camera has no recording at the clock time. The other cameras continue.
  • A tile is also black for a moment after a jump, until the correct frame is ready.

Control bar

ItemFunction

Scissors buttonStarts the bbox capture.
Camera buttonOpens the camera picker. The numbers show how many cameras are shown, for example 3/5.
Menu buttonOpen Folder, Close Folder, Settings, Help.

At 8x and 16x the app can show key frames only, so the picture changes in steps. The clock speed stays correct.

Go to a time

  1. Press G. The "Go to time" dialog opens with the clock time.
  2. Type a time as HH:mm:ss, HH:mm, or HHmmss.
  3. Press Enter or click OK. Press Esc or click Cancel to close the dialog with no jump.
  • You can also paste a time in the dialog. A pasted time must be HH:mm:ss or HHmmss. A correct time starts the jump immediately. Other text is ignored.
  • Shift + G jumps to the time in the clipboard with no dialog. If the clipboard does not hold a time as HH:mm:ss or HHmmss, nothing happens.
  • A time before the earliest time jumps to the earliest time. A time after the latest time jumps to the latest time.
  • If the recordings pass midnight, a time before the start time of day means the next day.

Copy the time

Click the timer or press T. Playback pauses, and the clock time goes to the clipboard as HH:mm:ss.

Choose the cameras

  1. Click the camera button or press C.
  2. Set or clear the checkbox of each camera. "Select all" and "Select none" change all checkboxes.
  3. Click OK or press Enter to apply. Click Cancel or press Esc to keep the cameras as they were.

The app does not read the files of a hidden camera. A camera that you show again starts at the clock time.

Bbox capture

Bbox capture copies an area of one camera frame to the clipboard as a picture.

  1. Click the scissors button or press F. Playback pauses.
  2. Move the pointer to a camera. The tile under the pointer gets a blue border.
  3. Click the camera. A dialog opens with the frame of that camera.
  4. Draw a rectangle on the picture. When you release the pointer, the area goes to the clipboard.
  5. Draw again to copy a different area, or click Close.
  • Before you click a camera, you can jump to a different time.
  • To stop the camera choice, press Esc or F, or click the scissors button again.
  • The picture has the shape that the grid uses. With the 16:9 video ratio, the picture and the copied area are stretched.

Settings

Open the menu button, then "Settings".

SettingOptionResult
Video ratio16:9Each video is stretched to 16:9. This is the default.
Video ratioNoneEach video keeps its own shape.

The setting returns to the default when the app starts.
